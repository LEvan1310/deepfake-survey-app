Participant Information & Informed Consent

Please read this information before taking part. This study is being conducted as part of a GUSTO College academic computing research assignment on political deepfakes, misinformation and audience trust.

Purpose of the studyThis project examines how AI-generated or manipulated political media can affect judgement, believability, trust in political information, and confidence in news or election-related content.
What you will doYou will answer background questions, view political/news-related media, classify video clips, respond to warning-label questions, and complete short reflection questions.
Estimated timeThe full study is expected to take approximately 15–20 minutes, depending on video playback and your response speed.
Voluntary participationTaking part is your choice. You may stop before submitting the survey if you no longer wish to continue.

How your responses will be used

  • Responses are collected for this GUSTO College academic research assignment and will be used for research analysis, reporting and academic evaluation.
  • The survey records the name you provide and also generates a participant ID so responses from different stages can be connected correctly.
  • When findings are summarized, responses should be discussed as research data rather than presented publicly as personally identifiable individual answers.
  • Your survey responses are not collected for advertising, marketing or commercial profiling.
  • Access to the research dashboard is restricted to the researcher/authorized academic review rather than ordinary survey participants.

What to expect

  • Some material may involve politicians, elections, warfare/news events, AI-generated media, manipulated media or potentially misleading claims.
  • There may be correct and incorrect answers in the educational video quiz, but your score is not a judgement of your intelligence, character or personal worth.
  • Please answer according to your genuine reaction. During the classification quiz, do not search online for the answer unless the study specifically asks you to verify a claim.
Your trust matters: this page explains why the data is collected and how it is used so you can make an informed choice before participating. If you do not agree, you do not need to continue with the study.
