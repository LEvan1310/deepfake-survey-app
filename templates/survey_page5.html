Before the Quiz: What is a Deepfake?

A deepfake is synthetic or manipulated audio, image or video created with AI so that a person appears to say or do something that did not happen in that form.

No single visual clue proves that a video is fake. Useful checks include the original source, reporting from multiple reliable sources, full context, audio/lip synchronization, lighting and frame-to-frame consistency.

Political and warfare deepfakes can mislead viewers, but genuine videos can also be reposted with false captions or missing context. Verify both authenticity and context before believing or sharing.
