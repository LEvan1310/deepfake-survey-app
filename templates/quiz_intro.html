🎬 Political Deepfake Video Assessment

You have completed the Political Deepfake Awareness & Verification pages. You will now apply that understanding while judging the study clips.

Instructions

  • Watch each political or news-related clip carefully.
  • Decide whether you think the clip is Real, AI-Generated, or if you are Not sure.
  • Rate how confident you are in your judgement and identify what most influenced it.
  • Use the awareness principles you have just reviewed, but answer according to your own genuine judgement.
  • Do not search for the study answer while completing the classification quiz.
Remember: one visual or audio artifact is not enough to prove that a clip is fake. Consider source, context, evidence and media clues together.
